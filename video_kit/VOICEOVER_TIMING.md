## Recording tips

1. Record each paragraph separately on a phone or computer microphone.
2. Leave one second of silence at the beginning and end of each clip.
3. Keep the voice calm and conversational; do not read the percentages too fast.
4. In Clipchamp or CapCut, place clips at `0:00`, `0:25`, `0:55`, `1:20`,
   `1:45`, and `2:10`.
5. Export MP4 at 1080p. Keep the final duration at or below 3 minutes.
6. Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**, then test the link in an incognito window.

The included MP4 has no voice or music by design, so the recorder can add their
own narration without fighting an existing soundtrack.
